slabware

a component library from 1997, give or take  ★  no rounded corners were harmed  ★  best viewed at 800×600  ★  under construction forever

Three materials. A slab stands up: flat color, a 2px ink border and a hard shadow. A key presses: a loud keycap with a thick ink lip. A well sinks: the shadow falls inside it. Link slabware.css, and slabware.js if you want tabs, menus, dialogs and toasts to work.

the rules

  1. Nothing is rounded. Not corners, not radios, not anything.
  2. Shadows are solid ink, down and to the right. No blur, no color, no spread.
  3. Pressing sinks toward the shadow. Hover pushes 2px in, active pushes all the way. Things never lift.
  4. Stand, press or sink. Slabs cast a shadow out, keys have a lip, wells hold their shadow inside. Pick one per thing.
  5. Motion steps. Animations use steps(), so they chunk along like a 1997 modem.
  6. Flat paint. Eleven solid colors and nothing else. Nothing fades, glows or goes see-through. Hover is always the same move: ink, with yellow type.
  7. Bungee shouts, mono talks. Bungee for names, headings and big numbers, Courier Prime for everything else.

line
trend over time: fat flat ribbons straight on the card, each casting a hard ink shadow, no outlines, no gridlines. each series ends in a chip of its own color with its latest value. shape: "steps" draws staircases. hover or tab in for the crosshair; the legend toggles series; the table key shows the numbers.
